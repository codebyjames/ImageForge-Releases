2. THE MODEL WEIGHTS ARE NOT PART OF THIS SOFTWARE
--------------------------------------------------

ImageForge ships no model weights. When you install the engine or choose a
model, the app downloads those weights directly from their publishers —
Stability AI, Black Forest Labs, and others — to your machine.

Your use of those weights is governed by THEIR licences, between you and
them. Some permit commercial use, some permit it only below a revenue
threshold, and at least one forbids it outright. They also carry use
restrictions of their own.

The app's System screen has a Licences panel listing what each model
permits, with a link to the full text of each licence. Read it before you
publish or sell anything you generate. It is a summary written in good faith
and checked against the publishers, not legal advice, and not a substitute
for the licence itself.

Two things that panel cannot cover, and you should know about:

  * Models you pull through Discover. Those come from anywhere on
    HuggingFace. Check the licence on the model's own page.
  * LoRAs you train or download. Nobody publishes terms for those. What you
    may do with the output depends on the images it was trained on, which
    only you know.

-----------------

The model licences behind this app — CreativeML OpenRAIL-M and OpenRAIL++
among them — carry use restrictions, and those restrictions travel to you as
a user of the weights. They are not this author's rules; they are the
publishers'. In substance, you may not use these models, or this app, to
generate or distribute content that:

  * sexualises children, in any form or depiction;
  * is created to harass, defame, threaten, or impersonate a real person,
    including sexual imagery of a real person made without their consent;
  * is presented as a genuine photograph or record of something that did not
    happen, in order to deceive;
  * incites violence or hatred against people on the basis of who they are;
  * provides instructions for seriously harming people;
  * violates any law that applies to you.

Read the licence linked beside each model for the authoritative wording. If
you disagree with these restrictions, the remedy is to not use the models —
not to use them anyway.


4. THE CONTENT FILTER
---------------------

Stable Diffusion and SDXL models ship with an NSFW safety checker, and
ImageForge leaves it ON by default.

It can be switched off by setting IMAGEFORGE_DISABLE_SAFETY_CHECKER=1. That
is a deliberate act. Turning it off does not change anything in section 3:
the restrictions come from the model licences, not from the filter, and
removing a filter does not remove a term.

The filter is imperfect in both directions. It misses things and it flags
harmless images. It is a safeguard, not a guarantee, and it is not a reason
to publish something without looking at it.


5. WHAT LEAVES YOUR MACHINE
---------------------------

Nothing, unless you ask for it. Specifically:

  * No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting.
  * Nothing is sent at startup.
  * Downloading the engine or a model contacts HuggingFace, because that is
    where the files are.
  * The Discover screen searches HuggingFace when you search.
  * The update check contacts GitHub, only when you press it, and sends
    nothing but the request itself.
  * If you configure RunPod for cloud rendering, your prompts and images go
    to RunPod under their terms, with your own API key.
